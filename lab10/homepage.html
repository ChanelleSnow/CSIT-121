Lab 10: Special Topics

PNG

PNG stands for Portable Network Graphics:

The PNG file format was created in 1995 by PNG Development Group, who goal was to make a image file better than GIF

PNG was designed for transferring images on the Internet, but it is also used for graphics in printed materials

Fun Fact:

PNG is officially pronounced ``ping'' (at least in English) but never spelled that way. "Believe it or not, in November 1998 the issue once again came under discussion, this time with greater emphasis on non-English
pronunciation. Though the ``three-letter'' approach (i.e., P-N-G spoken as three separate letters) was not approved for inclusion in the spec, it may be considered an acceptable unofficial alternative."-(W3C School)
